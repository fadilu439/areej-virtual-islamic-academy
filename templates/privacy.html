Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AREEJ Virtual Islamic Academy (“AREEJ”, “we”, “us” or “the Academy”) handles personal information when you visit our website, send an admissions enquiry, use an academy portal, or contact us through the website's live-chat or AI-assisted support service.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information you choose to provide, including a student's name and age, parent or guardian name, applicant name, selected programme, preferred class arrangements, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, city or country, messages sent to the Academy, and files you choose to upload (such as an admission or payment-support document).

When you use an account or portal, we may process account details such as username, role, and password hash, together with information entered or generated while using the relevant portal. We do not store passwords in readable form.

Our website and chat providers may also process technical information needed to deliver and secure their services, such as IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, cookies or similar identifiers, and basic usage or diagnostic data. The exact technical information depends on the service and your browser settings.

2. Live chat and AI-assisted support

The website may provide live chat and an AI-assisted agent through Tawk.to. When you open or use the chat, the messages and details you choose to share may be processed by Tawk.to and made available to authorised AREEJ personnel through the chat service. Tawk.to may also process technical information required to operate, secure and improve its service. Please review Tawk.to's own privacy information for details about its processing, retention and controls.

The AI agent is an automated support assistant. It is intended to answer common questions about AREEJ using information and guidance supplied by the Academy, including information about programmes, admissions and lesson arrangements. It is not a human staff member, teacher, scholar, medical professional or legal adviser. AI-generated answers may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect; important matters such as admission decisions, fees, payment confirmation, safeguarding concerns or individual student arrangements should be confirmed with authorised AREEJ staff.

If the agent cannot confidently answer a question, it should direct the visitor to the Academy's human support team rather than inventing an answer. Human staff may review chat messages to respond to enquiries, follow up on support requests, maintain service quality and protect the Academy and its users.

Please do not send passwords, one-time verification codes, bank-card details, account credentials, confidential documents, or information that is not necessary to answer your enquiry through the chat. Do not rely on the AI agent to submit an admission, confirm a payment, change an account, or make a binding decision unless the website explicitly provides and confirms that function.

3. How we use information

We use information for legitimate Academy purposes, including responding to enquiries, explaining programmes, processing admissions, communicating with applicants or guardians, assessing programme choices, arranging classes, supporting students and families, administering accounts and portals, reviewing payment information where relevant, providing customer support, maintaining website security, and meeting applicable legal or record-keeping obligations.

4. How information is stored and shared

Information submitted through the website may be processed by the Academy's application, database and file-storage services. Website hosting, live-chat, AI-assisted support and other technical providers may process information on the Academy's behalf or as described in their own privacy terms. Access to information should be limited to authorised Academy personnel and service providers who need it to perform their duties.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use admissions or chat information for unrelated advertising by the Academy. We may disclose information where necessary to provide a requested service, protect users or the Academy, comply with law, or respond to a valid legal request.

5. Children's information

AREEJ provides educational services to children as well as adults. A parent or legal guardian should submit or supervise an enquiry for a child and should share only information reasonably needed for the enquiry or educational service. Children should not provide passwords, contact details, private family information or other sensitive information to the AI agent or in public chat without a parent or guardian's involvement.

6. Retention and security

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, to provide Academy services, resolve enquiries, maintain appropriate educational and financial records, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between the Academy's systems and third-party providers; please consult the relevant provider's privacy information for its retention practices.

We use measures such as role-based access controls and password hashing where supported by the application. No website, transmission method or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use care when sharing information online.

7. Your privacy choices and requests

You may contact the Academy through the contact channels published on the AREEJ website to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where the Academy is not required to retain it. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity or authority before responding. Requests concerning information held by Tawk.to or another third-party provider may also need to be made directly to that provider under its privacy procedures.

8. Third-party services and links

The website may rely on third-party services, including hosting and live-chat providers. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy notices. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience; the Academy is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites it does not operate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, AI-assisted support, services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. The “Last updated” date indicates when this page was most recently revised.
