AREEJ Virtual Islamic Academy · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AREEJ Virtual Islamic Academy handles information submitted through the admissions enquiry form on this website.

Information we collect. When you submit an enquiry, the form may collect the student's name and age, selected programme, guardian or applicant name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, city or country, and an optional payment-proof file (JPG, PNG or PDF).

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to admission enquiries, communicate with applicants or guardians, review programme choices, support assessment and class placement, and check payment information when a payment proof is provided.

Files and account information

Information submitted through the form is processed by the AREEJ website and stored in the academy application's database and file-storage location configured by the academy. Portal account information, including usernames and password hashes, is used to provide role-based access to the student, parent, staff and administrator portals. Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords.

Access and sharing

Admission and student information should be accessed only by academy personnel who need it to handle admissions or provide academy services. The current website code does not include an advertising or data-sale feature. The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, and technical service providers may process data as needed to operate the website.

Retention and security

The academy should retain personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy or to meet legitimate record-keeping obligations, and then securely delete or anonymise it. The academy uses access-controlled portals and password hashing; however, no website or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

An applicant or guardian may contact the academy administration to ask about the personal information they submitted, request correction of inaccurate details, or ask for deletion where the academy is not required to retain the information. The academy may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Children's information

Because the academy serves children, a parent or legal guardian should submit or supervise an enquiry for a child and provide only information needed for the admission process.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
