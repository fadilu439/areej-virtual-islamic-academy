Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

AREEJ Virtual Islamic Academy (“AREEJ”, “we”, “our” or “the Academy”) respects the privacy of students, parents, applicants, teachers and other visitors. This policy explains the types of information our current website and learning portal are designed to collect and how that information is used.

1. Information you may provide

When you submit an admission enquiry, the form may collect the student's name, age, selected programme, guardian or applicant name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, city or country, and an optional payment-proof file (JPG, PNG or PDF).

When an account is created for use of the learning portal, the system stores a username, a password hash, a display name and the account role (administrator, staff, student or parent). The application is designed to store the password as a one-way hash rather than plain text.

When portal users communicate or use learning features, the system may store message text, message attachments, class information, attendance, assignment submissions, learning materials, assessment results and related academic records.

2. How we use information

  • To receive and respond to admission enquiries.
  • To review programme choices and payment evidence submitted with an enquiry.
  • To create and manage authorised student, parent, staff and administrator accounts.
  • To provide classes, learning materials, assignments, messages, attendance records and academic results.
  • To maintain the operation, security and administration of the Academy's website and learning portal.

3. Files and payment evidence

Admission enquiries may include an optional payment-proof image or PDF. Portal messages and learning activities may also include permitted attachments. Please submit only files relevant to the stated purpose and do not upload passwords, bank-card details or unrelated sensitive documents.

4. Who can access information

Information is intended to be used by authorised Academy personnel for admission, administration and educational purposes. Portal access is role-based in the application. Parents may be linked to student records so they can view relevant academic information. This policy does not claim that information is never processed by hosting, storage or infrastructure providers; the providers and settings may depend on the Academy's deployment configuration.

5. Third-party services

The public website currently loads web fonts from Google Fonts. When a browser requests those fonts, the font provider may receive standard connection information such as the browser's IP address. The application code reviewed for this policy does not show Google sign-in or an online card-payment checkout. If these or other third-party services are added, this policy should be updated.

6. Storage, security and retention

Information and uploaded files are stored using the database and file-storage configuration selected for the Academy's deployment. The application includes password hashing, session protections and request-forgery checks, but no website can promise absolute security. Access to administrator accounts and deployment credentials should be restricted.

The current application code does not define an automatic deletion schedule for admission enquiries, messages, attachments or academic records. The Academy should retain information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for education, administration, legal obligations or dispute resolution, and establish a process for securely deleting information that is no longer required.

7. Children and parents

AREEJ serves children as well as adults. A parent or guardian should provide a child's information where appropriate and should contact the Academy administration if they have questions about a child's information or wish to request a correction.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact the Academy administration to ask what personal information has been submitted, request correction of inaccurate details, or ask for deletion where the Academy is not required to keep the information. We may need to verify the identity and authority of the person making a request before acting on it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, learning portal, storage arrangements or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AREEJ Virtual Islamic Academy administration through the Academy's official contact channel. The website currently directs admission and portal-support enquiries to the Academy administration.
